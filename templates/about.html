What is Talbeena?

Talbeena is a nourishing barley-based porridge mentioned in the Sunnah of Prophet Muhammad (SAW). We bring this blessed tradition to modern homes with purity, care, and authentic ingredients.

🌾

Pure Barley

We source the finest barley grains, stone-ground to preserve their natural goodness, fiber, and minerals.

🤲

Prophetic Tradition

Talbeena is a Sunnah food recommended by Prophet Muhammad (SAW) for its healing and strengthening properties.

💚

Natural Healing

Rich in beta-glucan fiber, talbeena supports heart health, digestion, and provides calm, sustained energy.

🏠

For the Whole Family

From children to elders, our blends are crafted for every member of your household to enjoy and benefit from.

Our Mission

Talbeena has been cherished for over 1,400 years in Islamic tradition. Made from barley flour mixed with water or milk, it is a simple yet profoundly nourishing food — gentle on the stomach, comforting to the heart, and loved by generations.

Our mission is to revive this beautiful Sunnah and make it accessible to Muslim families worldwide. We offer pure talbeena in convenient, modern formats — without compromising on authenticity, quality, or the spiritual intention behind this blessed food.
