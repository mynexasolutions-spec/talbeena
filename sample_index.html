What is Talbeena?

A Healing Porridge
Loved for Centuries

Talbeena (التلبينة) is a traditional barley-based porridge that has been treasured across the Muslim world for over 1,400 years. Made by simmering barley flour with water or milk and a touch of honey, it resembles milk in its soft whiteness — and in its gentleness on the body.

Modern nutrition now confirms what generations already knew — barley is a powerhouse of fiber, vitamins, and minerals. Whether you seek comfort, energy, or healing, Talbeena delivers.

How to Prepare

Simple. Warm.
Ready in Minutes.

No complicated instructions. Just pure nourishment prepared with love.

1

Add Barley Flour

Mix 2–3 tablespoons of our pure barley flour into a cup of cold water or milk until smooth.

2

Simmer Gently

Pour into a small pot and heat on low flame, stirring continuously for 5–7 minutes until it thickens.

3

Sweeten with Honey

Remove from heat and stir in a spoonful of pure natural honey for extra nourishment.

4

Serve Warm & Enjoy

Pour into your favourite bowl and savour each healing sip. Best enjoyed in the morning.
