What is Talbeena?

Talbeena is a nourishing barley-based porridge mentioned in the Sunnah of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. Made from barley flour mixed with water or milk, it is a simple yet profoundly healing food — gentle on the stomach, comforting to the heart, and loved by generations.

Rich in beta-glucan fiber, natural vitamins, and minerals, talbeena supports heart health, digestion, and provides calm, sustained energy throughout the day.
